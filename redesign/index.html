Something else on your mind? Ask on WhatsApp

How much does a song cost?

Studio time is ₹1,200 an hour with an engineer. Recording to Final is ₹14,000 per song and Production to Final is ₹26,000 per song. Mixing or mastering on its own starts at ₹2,000 — see the full rates.

I've never recorded before. Is that okay?

That's completely fine. Most of our clients have recorded 0–3 times before. The engineer walks you through mic technique, your headphone mix and every take.

What should I bring?

Your voice and your lyrics. Send your beat or a reference track the day before so we can set up.

Can I bring my own beat?

Yes — MP3, WAV or a DAW project. Send it in advance.

What's a stem mix?

Instead of mixing your vocal over a finished stereo beat, we balance every part of the song — drums, bass, melody, vocals — on its own. It's how released songs are mixed.

How do booking and cancellation work?

A 20% advance confirms your slot. Reschedule free with 24+ hours' notice. Same-day cancellations forfeit the advance. If we cancel, you get a full refund.

Do prices include GST?

Individual artists pay exactly the listed price. If you're booking as a GST-registered business, GST is added at the applicable rate and shown separately on your invoice.

Where are you, and is there parking?

Near ICICI Bank, Sector 19, Airoli — 8 minutes from Airoli Station on the Trans-Harbour line. Free parking outside.
