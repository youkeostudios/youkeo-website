Trk 03FAQ

Before you pay

Do I pay the full amount upfront?

No. A 20% advance confirms the booking. The balance is collected after the session or on delivery of files.

How long does a recording session take?

Typically 2–3 hours: ₹2,400–₹3,600 at ₹1,200/hr. Most songs need about 2 hours; allow 3–4 for multiple songs.

What if my session runs over time?

Extra hours are billed at ₹1,200/hr. We always check with you before extending, so there are no surprises.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Reschedule free up to 24 hours before your session. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice forfeit the 20% advance. If we cancel, you get a full refund.

Can you mix songs recorded elsewhere?

Yes. Send stems or a stereo rough for a quote. Stereo mixes from ₹5,000; stem mixes from ₹12,000. Includes 2 revision rounds and streaming-ready masters.

Do you do EP or album packages?

Yes. For an EP (3+ songs) or an album, message us and we'll put together a custom package for the project.

Are there any hidden charges?

None. Individual artists pay exactly the listed price. GST-registered businesses pay GST at the applicable rate, shown separately on the invoice.
