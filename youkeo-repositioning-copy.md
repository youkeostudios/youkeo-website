## THE CORE FIX (do this first — it's bigger than any single line)

Your site currently says two opposite things:
- **Recording page:** "This studio isn't for everyone… ✗ looking for the cheapest rate" — premium, worth-it, anti-cheap. **This is the right positioning.**
- **Home hero:** "Book Your Session — 30% Off" + "₹800/hr first-time" — discount-led, cheap-signal. **This fights the above.**

**Decision (make it consciously): commit to PREMIUM across every page.** It's what we locked (serious releasing artists, value-doubt fear, "worth it" not "cheap"), and your best page already lives there. Everything below assumes premium. Once you choose it, the discount framing has to go — you can't be "not the cheapest" and "30% off" at once.

## WHAT TO CHANGE (with copy)

### HOME — hero
- **CTA:** ~~"Book Your Session — 30% Off"~~ → **"Check Availability"** (secondary text link: "↓ Hear the work"). Kills the cheap signal; lowers commitment friction.
- **Subhead:** ~~"Pro-grade studio. 30 minutes from Mumbai."~~ → **"A pro-grade room, an easy team, and a track that actually competes. Airoli, Navi Mumbai."**
  - (Ties premium+comfort to the outcome, and fixes the distance inconsistency — see factual fixes.)
- If you want a live offer, put it as a thin banner strip ("Monsoon slots open — limited"), never as the CTA.

### HOME — stats bar
- Clarify what "1M+ Spotify streams" refers to. Right now it's ambiguous (studio total? one track? the founder's?). If it's a client track, label it "1M+ streams on tracks recorded here." Honesty + it's actually stronger when specific.

### PRICING — recording tier
- Reframe the intro-rate framing. ~~"First-Time Session · Introductory rate for new clients ₹800/hr"~~ reads as a discount, and pairing it with "Repeat Sessions ₹1,200/hr" means **returning artists pay MORE than newcomers** — that's backwards and can quietly annoy loyal clients.
  - **Recommended:** one clear rate. **"Recording — ₹1,200/hr. First session ₹800/hr so you can try the room risk-free."** Frames the lower price as a *trial*, not a discount, and doesn't punish repeat clients.
- Keep everything else on this page — the transparency framing is a genuine asset.

### RELEASE & PROMOTION — reframe, don't cut (correcting my earlier advice)
- I earlier said "cut it." On re-reading, I was wrong: your TG is *releasing* artists, and distribution + playlist pitching directly serves the "get my release heard" goal. It's on-positioning.
- The problem is only the **agency tone** ("Meta Ad management"). Reframe as artist-support, not a marketing service:
  - Header stays: **"Get your music in front of real ears."** (good)
  - Keep **"100% royalties retained by you"** front and centre — that's artist-friendly and rare.
  - Soften "Meta Ad management" → **"paid promotion that brings real listeners, not bot streams"** (you already say this — lead with it, drop the corporate label).

### CTA consistency (whole site)
- You currently mix "Book a Session," "Book Your Session — 30% Off," "Chat with us," "Book Your Recording Session." Standardize the primary to **"Check Availability"** everywhere, routing to your WhatsApp/booking flow. One label, repeated. (If your booking is purely WhatsApp-conversational, "Check Availability" still works as the button; it just opens WhatsApp.)

### RECORDING page
- Mostly keep — it's your best page. One line: "an engineer who's put tracks on New Music Friday" is strong **only if literally true** (see verify list). If it's true, keep; if it's a stretch, cut it — a false specific claim is worse than none.
