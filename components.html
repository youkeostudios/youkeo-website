FAQ

What is Pianity all about, and how is it going to change my music experience?

Pianity connects music lovers with limited-edition songs and new ways to support the artists they follow.

What makes Pianity stand out from other music platforms?

Each release is designed to be collectible, connecting ownership with a closer artist community.

What’s this ‘Toplist’ everyone’s buzzing about, and how can I join in?

The Toplist showcases activity and new discoveries across the community.

I’ve heard of ‘Scarcity Levels’. What do they mean for my music collection’s value?

Scarcity reflects the number of editions available for each song and the perks attached to it.

What are these $PIA tokens, and how do I earn and use them?

Tokens support participation in the platform’s artist and collector ecosystem.

What’s a ‘Bounty’ and how do I claim one?

Bounties are community rewards made available through specific activities and releases.

Tell me about the ‘Clubs’ on Pianity. How can they bring me closer to my favorite artists?

Artist clubs unlock more direct ways to join an artist’s world.

What are these Leaderboards I’ve heard about, and how can I climb to the top?

Leaderboards recognise active participation from listeners and collectors.

What’s the scoop on these Weekly Rewards, and how can I boost my earnings?

Weekly rewards offer recurring opportunities to take part in the community.

How do payments work on Pianity? Can I use my credit card or crypto, and what about gas fees?

Payment options and any transaction fees are shown before a purchase is completed.
