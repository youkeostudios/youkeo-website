Trk 06FAQ

Before you book

How long is a session?

Typically 2–3 hours: ₹2,400–₹3,600 at ₹1,200/hr. Most songs need about 2 hours; allow 3–4 for multiple songs.

What should I bring?

Your voice and lyrics. Send any beat or reference track the day before so we can prepare.

Can I bring my own beat?

Yes. MP3, WAV, or a supported DAW project. Send it in advance.

What's your cancellation policy?

A 20% advance confirms booking. Reschedule free with 24+ hours' notice. Same-day cancellations forfeit the advance. If we cancel, you get a full refund.

How do I get there?

Sector 19, Airoli, 8 minutes from Airoli Station. By road: Thane ~20 minutes, Vashi ~25, Powai/Mulund 15–25, depending on traffic. Free parking outside.

Will my track sound professional?

Tracks recorded here have passed 10M+ combined streams and reached editorial playlists, including New Music Friday India. Expect a Neumann U87, treated acoustics, and an engineer experienced in major releases.

Can you mix recordings made elsewhere?

Yes. Send stems or a stereo rough for a quote. Stereo mixes from ₹5,000; stem mixes from ₹12,000. Includes 2 revision rounds and streaming-ready masters.
