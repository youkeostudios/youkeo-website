02Service Description

Youkeo Studios provides professional recording studio services including vocal recording, instrument recording, music production, mixing, and mastering. Recording sessions take place at our studio in Airoli, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra. Mixing and mastering can also be done on recordings you send us.

The quality of our services depends on factors including your preparation, the quality of your material, and the technical expertise of our team.

03Booking & Reservation Policy

Making a Booking:

  • Bookings can be made via WhatsApp (+91 9619192992), phone call or email (hello@youkeostudios.com)
  • A booking requires your name, phone number and session date preference, and is confirmed once the 20% advance is paid
  • We recommend booking 24–48 hours in advance to secure your preferred time slot
  • Availability is on a first-come, first-served basis

Confirmation:

  • You will receive a booking confirmation via email or WhatsApp
  • Keep your booking confirmation for reference

04Pricing & Payment

Rates:

  • Standard recording session: ₹1,200/hour (engineer included); additional hours are billed at the same rate
  • Record & Mix package: ₹7,500 per song (2-hour recording session, vocal tuning, stereo mixing & mastering, 1 revision)
  • Recording to Final package: ₹14,000 per song (2-hour recording session, vocal tuning, stem mixing & mastering, 2 revisions)
  • Production to Final package: ₹26,000 per song (custom beat and arrangement, 2-hour recording session, tuning, stem mixing & mastering, release consultation)
  • Music Production: ₹12,000 per song
  • Vocal Tuning: ₹1,500 per song
  • Stereo Mixing & Mastering: Starting from ₹5,000
  • Stem Mixing & Mastering: Starting from ₹12,000
  • Master & Polish (mastering only): ₹2,000 per track
  • EP and album projects (3+ songs): Custom quote

Payment Terms:

  • A 20% advance confirms your booking. The balance is due after the session or on delivery of files, unless otherwise agreed
  • We accept cash and digital payment methods
  • All prices are in Indian Rupees (₹)
  • Individual artists pay the listed price. GST-registered businesses pay GST at the applicable rate, shown separately on the invoice

05Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Cancellations:

  • 24+ hours notice: Full refund of any payment made
  • Less than 24 hours notice: The 20% advance is forfeited
  • No-show: The full session fee will be charged
  • If we cancel: Full refund of any payment made

Rescheduling:

  • You can reschedule your session free of charge with at least 24 hours notice
  • Subject to availability at your preferred new date/time
