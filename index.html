Recording is ₹1,200/hr (engineer included). We also offer two complete packages: Recording to Final (₹14,000 per song; includes 2 hours of recording + mixing & mastering) and Production to Final (₹26,000 per song; includes custom beat production + 2 hours of recording + mixing & mastering).

We're at Near ICICI Bank, Sector-19, Airoli, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra 400708. Just 8 minutes from Airoli Railway Station on the Harbour Line. Free parking is available nearby.

Completely. Most of our clients have recorded 0–3 times before arriving. Our engineer walks you through everything — mic technique, headphone mix, takes, and direction. The studio is built to make you comfortable so you can do your best work.

We use industry-standard equipment including the Neumann U87 condenser microphone — one of the most trusted vocal mics in professional studios worldwide. Our control room is fully acoustically treated and equipped with professional monitoring and processing gear.

Yes. Standalone Stereo Mixing & Mastering starts at ₹5,000, and full Multitrack / Stem Mixing & Mastering starts at ₹12,000. We also offer mastering-only (Master & Polish) for ₹2,000. All files are delivered streaming-ready for platforms like Spotify and Apple Music.
